About This Wine

1969 original vineyard establishment plantings of Shiraz and Marsanne, with additional Shiraz planted across the 1990's.

Fruit was hand-picked, chilled overnight, sorted and destemmed directly to the Yarra Yering half-tonne open fermenters.

Complex and layered; sweet and savoury all at once. The fragrance is a melting pot of violets, cranberry, plums and fragrant spice.
